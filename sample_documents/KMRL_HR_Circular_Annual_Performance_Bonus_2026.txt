SUBJECT: ANNUAL PERFORMANCE APPRAISAL & FESTIVE EX-GRATIA BONUS SCHEME (2026-27)
================================================================================

1. GENERAL DIRECTIVE
The Competent Authority of Kochi Metro Rail Limited is pleased to announce the Annual Performance Incentive and Onam Ex-Gratia Scheme for the financial year 2026–27. This applies to all permanent, deputation, and long-term contract personnel who have completed at least 180 continuous days of operational service as of August 31, 2026.

2. PERFORMANCE-LINKED INCENTIVE (PLI) SLABS
Eligibility is determined based on the Annual Key Performance Index (KPI) evaluated by Department Heads:
- Grade A1 (Exceptional - KPI >= 90%): 22% of Basic Pay + Performance Memento
- Grade A2 (Exceeds Expectations - KPI 75% to 89%): 18% of Basic Pay
- Grade B1 (Meets Standards - KPI 60% to 74%): 14% of Basic Pay
- Grade B2 (Under Review - KPI < 60%): Conditional eligibility subject to completion of mandatory retraining program at Muttom Metro Academy.

3. SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR SAFETY & ROTATING SHIFT STAFF
- Train Operators and Station Controllers with ZERO safety violations or SPAD (Signal Passed at Danger) incidents will receive an additional Safety Allowance of ₹12,500.
- Night Maintenance and Permanent Way Engineers are eligible for an Overtime Discretionary Stipend in accordance with Kerala Industrial Relations Guidelines.

4. DISBURSEMENT SCHEDULE
- Performance rating submissions due: September 5, 2026
- HR Verification & Internal Audit: September 10, 2026
- Direct Credit to Employee Salary Accounts: September 15, 2026

5. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Employees who wish to file an appeal regarding their performance evaluation band may submit an online grievance through the KMRL IDMS Helpdesk portal within 10 working days of score publication.
