1. PROJECT TITLE
Design, Engineering, Supply, Installation, Testing, and Commissioning of 2.5 MWp Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic (SPV) Power Plant at Muttom Maintenance Depot and Administrative Complex, including 10 Years Comprehensive Operation & Maintenance (O&M).

2. KEY PARAMETERS & CRITICAL DATES
- Estimated Project Cost: INR 11,85,00,000/- (Eleven Crores Eighty-Five Lakhs Only)
- Earnest Money Deposit (EMD): INR 11,85,000/- (via Bank Guarantee / Online NEFT)
- Tender Document Fee: INR 15,000/- (Non-Refundable)
- Tender Publishing Date: October 15, 2026
- Pre-Bid Meeting (Hybrid): October 28, 2026 at 11:00 AM (KMRL HQ, Revenue Tower)
- Last Date for Online Bid Submission: November 18, 2026, 15:00 HRS
- Technical Bid Opening Date: November 19, 2026, 15:30 HRS

3. MINIMUM TECHNICAL QUALIFICATIONS
3.1 The bidder must have successfully commissioned at least one single grid-tied rooftop solar power project of minimum 1.5 MWp capacity for any Metro Rail, Indian Railways, or Central/State PSU in the past 5 years.
3.2 The solar modules must be Tier-1 Mono-PERC bifacial panels certified as per IEC 61215 and IEC 61730 standards with a minimum efficiency rating of 21.5%.
3.3 Inverters must adhere to IEEE 1547 and possess zero-export dynamic feed limiters compliant with KSEBL grid codes.

4. SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS
Bids must be uploaded strictly through the Kerala Government e-Procurement portal. Hardcopy submissions of Technical Bids must be sealed and delivered to the Executive Director (Projects & Electrical), KMRL Corporate Office, 4th Floor, JLN Stadium Complex, Kochi.
