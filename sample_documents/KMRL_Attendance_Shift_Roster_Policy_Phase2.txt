================================================================================
KOCHI METRO RAIL LIMITED (KMRL) - OPERATIONS CONTROL & WORKFORCE POLICY
DIRECTIVE NO: KMRL/OPS/ROSTER/2026/088
SUBJECT: 3-TIER OPERATIONAL SHIFT ROSTER & BIOMETRIC ATTENDANCE COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

1. APPLICABILITY & SCOPE
This directive applies to all frontline station operations staff, including Station Controllers (SCs), Train Operators (TOs), Customer Facilitation Officers (CFOs), and Rolling Stock Technical Inspection teams across Phase 1 & Phase 2 corridors.

2. STANDARD THREE-TIER SHIFT TIMINGS
To guarantee uninterrupted passenger service from 06:00 AM to 11:00 PM across all 25 active metro stations, personnel are rostered into three continuous duty shifts:
- Shift 1 (Morning Shift): 05:30 HRS to 13:30 HRS
- Shift 2 (Evening Shift): 13:30 HRS to 21:30 HRS
- Shift 3 (Night Maintenance & Stabling): 21:30 HRS to 05:30 HRS

3. HANDOVER PROTOCOLS & MANDATORY BREATH ANALYZER TEST
3.1 All Train Operators and Station Controllers must report at their assigned station depot or crew room at least 20 minutes prior to scheduled duty commencement.
3.2 Every Train Operator must clear an automated digital Breath Analyzer (BA) alcohol screening before taking charge of a rake cab. Any reading > 0.00 mg/100ml results in immediate grounding and inquiry.
3.3 Relieving crew must physically verify logbook entries, PSD interlocks, and emergency brake status before signing the digital handover manifest.

4. BIOMETRIC ATTENDANCE & GRACE PERIOD
- Morning Shift Grace Period: Up to 05:40 HRS (maximum 3 occurrences per month).
- Late arrival beyond grace periods will result in automatic half-day Casual Leave deduction.
- Absence without prior approval for more than 48 consecutive hours will trigger an official inquiry under KMRL Disciplinary Regulations.

Approved by: General Manager (Operations), Kochi Metro Rail Limited.
Effective Date: November 1, 2026.
