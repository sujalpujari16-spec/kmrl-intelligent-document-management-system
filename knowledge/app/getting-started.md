# KMRL IDMS - Getting Started Guide

The KMRL Intelligent Document Management System (IDMS) is the centralized portal for Kochi Metro Rail Limited employees, HR managers, and administrators.

## Key Features & Modules
1. **Document Management:** Upload, browse, search, and manage KMRL circulars, policies, scanned forms, and tenders.
2. **AI Assistant:** An intelligent conversational assistant powered by OpenAI GPT, pgvector RAG, and LangGraph Supervisor that answers policy queries, application how-to instructions, and live employee status.
3. **Intelligent Search:** Hybrid semantic vector and keyword search across all indexed KMRL PDF and image files.
4. **Approvals & Workflows:** Real-time review queue for HR officers and admins to approve or reject employee-submitted documents.
5. **Audit & Activity Logs:** Complete immutable audit stream of all user actions, document uploads, and backend AI activities.
6. **Reports & Analytics:** Live analytics on repository volume, category distribution, and approval bottlenecks.
7. **User Management:** Staff profile directory, role assignments, and photo management.
