# Document Approval Workflow & Review Guide

## Understanding the Approval Process
1. **Submission:** When an employee uploads an application, leave slip, or circular draft, it enters the system with status `Pending Approval`.
2. **Review:** HR Managers and Administrators receive real-time notifications and see the pending counter in the **Approvals & Workflows** tab.
3. **Decision:**
   - **Approve:** The reviewer can add optional review notes and click **"Approve Document"**. The status changes to `Approved`, the document is indexed into the RAG vector search, and an instant notification is sent.
   - **Reject:** The reviewer provides feedback reasons and clicks **"Reject Document"**. The employee is notified to re-upload.
4. **Audit Trail:** Every approval or rejection timestamp, reviewer persona, and note are recorded permanently in **Audit & Activity Logs**.

## Why Is My Document Still Pending?
- Document uploads by general employees require HR verification before becoming universally accessible.
- If pending, your departmental reviewer has not completed their verification check yet. You can track this under **Approvals & Workflows**.
