# KMRL Leave Application & Verification Guide

## How to Apply for Leave in KMRL IDMS
1. Navigate to **Leave Management** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **"Apply for Leave"** button in the top right.
3. In the application form:
   - Select your **Leave Type** (Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned Leave, Maternity Leave, Paternity Leave).
   - Pick the **Start Date** and **End Date** (the system automatically calculates the calendar days).
   - Enter the detailed **Reason for Leave**.
   - If taking Sick / Medical Leave for more than 2 consecutive days, check the box to attach your physician's medical certificate.
4. Click **"Submit Application"**.

## What Happens Next
- Your request immediately enters the **Approvals & Workflows** queue for your departmental HR Officer.
- Its status is initially set to `Pending Approval`.
- Once HR verifies and approves your application, the status updates to `Approved`, you receive an instant notification, and your leave quota card automatically updates.

## How to Check Leave Status & Balances
- View your quota cards directly at the top of **Leave Management** (Casual: 7/12 left, Sick: 8/10 left, Earned: 24/30 left).
- Or ask the **AI Assistant**: *"How many leaves do I have left?"* or *"Show my pending leave request"*.

## Official KMRL Leave Entitlements
- **Casual Leave (CL):** 12 days per calendar year (max 3-4 consecutive days).
- **Sick Leave (SL):** 10 days per calendar year (medical certificate required for >2 days).
- **Earned Leave (EL):** 30 days per calendar year (accumulable up to 300 days and encashable).
- **Maternity Leave:** 180 consecutive calendar days fully paid for female employees.
- **Paternity Leave:** 15 calendar days fully paid for male employees.
